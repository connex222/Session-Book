Why this library exists

Most drill collections online give you a diagram and a title and leave you to work out the rest. That is fine if you already know what you are looking at. It is no use at all if you have twenty players arriving in ten minutes and one thing you want to fix.

Every session here comes from sessions actually run with real squads across eighteen years — under-10s who cannot yet receive on the half-turn, and open-age players who can but do not always choose to. Each one states what it is for, how to set it up, the four things worth stopping the practice for, and three ways to make it harder once the group has it. Nothing else.

If a session here does not work for your group, change the space before you change anything else. That single piece of advice has fixed more practices than any diagram.

How the sessions are organised

Fifty sessions across six groups: foundation practices every player needs, intermediate and advanced work for more experienced groups, touch-restricted play for sharpening tempo, small-group games for low numbers, and goalkeeping. Filter by level or theme on the main library page.
