# The Session Book V8

V8 builds on V7 and adds **coaching pathways**.

## What's new
- Seven pathway pages covering Possession & Build-Up, Defending & Pressing, Attacking & Finishing, Transition & Counter, Set Pieces & Restarts, Technical & Touch, and Goalkeeping.
- A central `pathways.html` directory.
- Every drill page now shows a **What to do next** panel with related sessions from its pathway.
- Drill pages link directly to their pathway.
- Pathway pages link back into the full drills and the session planner.
- Homepage/footer navigation now exposes Coaching pathways.
- Existing V7 planner, responsive layout, saved plans, notes and Back to top behaviour are retained.
- Sitemap regenerated for the expanded site.

## Coaching philosophy
The pathways are intentionally presented as a suggested route rather than a rigid curriculum. Coaches can start where their players are and move to the next challenge when the behaviour becomes reliable.

## Deployment
The site remains static and GitHub Pages compatible. No server or database is required.
