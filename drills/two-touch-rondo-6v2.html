Two-Touch Rondo — 6v2

In Possession · Two Touch · 6v2

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Keep the ball under real pressure with a strict two-touch limit, so the first touch has to do the thinking and the second only has to deliver.

Setup

  • 14m x 14m square, six players on the outside against two defenders inside
  • Strict two-touch maximum; a third touch is a turnover
  • Defenders swap out with whoever gave the ball away

Key coaching points

  • First touch away from the nearest defender, into the passing lane you have chosen
  • Support angles constantly — if you can see the ball on a straight line, move
  • Play the pass the defenders have just stopped being able to reach
  • Speak before the ball arrives, not as it does

Progressions

  • Reduce the area by two metres each time the group reaches ten passes
  • Add a bonus point for a pass split between the two defenders
  • Move to one touch for the outside players, two for a central floater
