The Touch Ladder

Full Pitch · Three / Two / One · 8v8

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Force the tempo to rise as the team moves up the pitch, by cutting the touch allowance in every zone the ball travels into.

Setup

  • Full-width area split into three zones: three touches, two touches, then one touch
  • 8v8 with goalkeepers; the touch limit is set by the zone the ball is in, not the player
  • A touch over the limit hands possession to the opposition in that zone

Key coaching points

  • Scan before receiving — in the one-touch zone the decision has to be already made
  • Support closer as the touch limit drops; distance kills a one-touch phase
  • Play into feet in the three-touch zone and into space in the one-touch zone
  • If the picture is not on, recycle back into a zone with more time

Progressions

  • Swap the ladder around so the tightest restriction is in your own third
  • Allow one “free touch” per team per attack, called by the player using it
  • Play the same game with no restriction and ask what changed
