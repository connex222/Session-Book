2v1 in the Channel

In Possession · Attacking Third · 2v1

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Commit the defender and then punish them — recognise whether the picture calls for the pass, the overlap, or simply going past on your own.

Setup

  • 20m x 14m channel with a small goal on the end line
  • Two attackers against one defender, starting from the halfway mark of the channel
  • The defender scores by winning the ball and dribbling out of the channel

Key coaching points

  • Run at the defender to commit them — a pass played before they engage solves nothing
  • The supporting player stays wide and level, not ahead of the ball
  • If the defender turns their hips to the runner, go past them yourself
  • The pass is released the moment the defender plants their weight

Progressions

  • Add a recovering defender who starts five yards behind
  • Reduce the channel width to make the overlap harder to find
  • Play 3v2 with the same principles and a full goal
