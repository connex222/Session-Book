The Sweeper-Keeper — Ball in Behind

Out of Possession · Starting Position

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Diagram

Objective

Read the ball played in behind early and get to it first, so the defensive line can hold its height instead of dropping every time the opposition look long.

Setup

  • High defensive line with the goalkeeper starting on the edge of the six-yard box
  • A server plays balls over the top for a striker to chase
  • Vary the weight so some are the keeper’s and some genuinely are not

Key coaching points

  • Start position moves with the ball — higher when the line is high, never on your line
  • Decide as the ball is struck, not as it bounces; hesitation loses the race
  • Come and claim or clear decisively; if it is not yours, get set and defend the shot instead
  • Communicate the decision so the defenders know whether to turn or hold

Common mistakes

  • The goalkeeper starts from a poor set position.
  • The first movement is late because the goalkeeper watches the ball rather than reading the situation.
  • The recovery action is forgotten after the first save or intervention.

What good looks like

  • The goalkeeper is balanced and set before the decisive action.
  • Footwork gets the body into the best position before the save, claim or distribution.
  • The goalkeeper finishes the action ready for the next phase.

Coach's language

  • “Set.”
  • “See it early.”
  • “Get your feet there first.”
  • “Recover for the next action.”

Progressions

  • Vary the service so half the balls are not the goalkeeper’s to take
  • Add a second striker so the clearance has to go somewhere sensible
  • Ask the goalkeeper to clear first-time into a target zone

Age groups and numbers

Best fit: U12–Adult · Group: 1–8

Adapt the work to the goalkeeper’s experience and keep the next action visible.

Coach's eye

Don't coach everything at once. Pick the one or two behaviours that matter most, then change the space or pressure before adding more rules.
