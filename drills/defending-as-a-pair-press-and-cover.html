Defending as a Pair – Press & Cover

Out of Possession • Defensive Third • 2v2

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Coordinate the two most basic defensive roles — the presser and the cover — so the pair defends as a unit rather than as two individuals.

Setup

  • 25m x 18m area with a goal and goalkeeper at one end, two cone gates at the other
  • Two attackers play against two defenders who start level with each other
  • Defenders score by winning the ball and dribbling through either gate

Key coaching points

  • Ball-near defender presses to delay, angled to force the attacker away from goal
  • Ball-far defender drops to cover: close enough to help, wide enough to see both
  • Say the roles out loud — “press” and “I've got cover” — every repetition
  • Swap roles the instant the ball is switched; the cover becomes the presser

Progressions

  • Make it 3v2 to stretch the pair and force earlier decisions
  • Condition the pair to win the ball within 8 seconds
  • Widen the area so a switch of play tests the recovery of both defenders
