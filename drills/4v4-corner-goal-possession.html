4v4 Corner-Goal Possession

In Possession · Small-Sided · 4v4

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Keep the ball with a purpose — four corner targets mean possession always has somewhere to go, and the team has to recognise which corner is actually open.

Setup

  • 28m x 28m square with a small gate in each corner
  • 4v4; a team scores by dribbling through any of the four gates under control
  • A gate cannot be used twice in a row by the same team

Key coaching points

  • Look for the corner furthest from the ball — that is usually the open one
  • Create the angle before calling for the ball, not after
  • One player always supports behind the ball so the team can turn play around
  • Switch early; a switch played one pass too late is a turnover

Progressions

  • Reduce to two touches for everyone
  • Add a neutral player who always joins the team in possession
  • Count consecutive passes before a gate is used and set a minimum
