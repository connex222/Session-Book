Full Phase of Play – Build to Finish

Full Pitch • Game Model Rehearsal

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Diagram

Objective

Rehearse the complete attacking sequence — build-up, progression through midfield, and finishing — as one continuous phase of play against realistic opposition.

Setup

  • Full pitch (or 9v9 on three-quarters) divided conceptually into Build, Progress, and Finish zones
  • 9v9 or 11v11 with functional roles matching the team's actual system of play
  • Opposition set up in a realistic mid-block to press build-up and defend the box

Key coaching points

  • Build zone: patience and secure possession, using the goalkeeper as an outlet
  • Progress zone: switch play and use third-man combinations to break the opposition's shape
  • Finish zone: quality of the final pass or cross matched by runners arriving with intent
  • Every phase links to the next — a poor first pass undermines the whole sequence

Common mistakes

  • Players stand behind defenders instead of creating clear passing lines.
  • The ball moves faster than the support, leaving the receiver isolated.
  • Players force a forward pass when the better decision is to secure possession and move the opponent.

What good looks like

  • The player on the ball has at least two useful options.
  • Support arrives at useful angles and distances, not simply close to the ball.
  • Players recognise when to play forward and when to recycle.

Coach's language

  • “Can you give me two options?”
  • “Open your body before the ball arrives.”
  • “Can we move them before we play through?”
  • “Secure it, then progress.”

Progressions

  • Add a time or touch limit in the Build zone to increase tempo
  • Condition that every move must involve at least one switch of play before entering the Finish zone
  • Play fully live with normal rules once the sequence is consistently well executed

Age groups and numbers

Best fit: U14–Adult · Group: 8–22+

Increase realism by linking the practice to the next phase rather than simply adding constraints.

Coach's eye

Don't coach everything at once. Pick the one or two behaviours that matter most, then change the space or pressure before adding more rules.

Session rhythm

  • 3 min: explain, demo and rehearse
  • 12–15 min: main practice

Coach's approach
