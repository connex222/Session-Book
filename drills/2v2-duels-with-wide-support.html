2v2 Duels with Wide Support

In Possession & Out of Possession · 2v2 (+2)

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Win your individual duel with a get-out available — attackers learn when to use the wide player, defenders learn to defend the pair rather than the ball.

Setup

  • 24m x 20m area with two small goals and a neutral wide player on each touchline
  • Two against two inside; the neutral players are limited to two touches and cannot score
  • Every attack must involve a neutral before a goal counts

Key coaching points

  • Attack the defender who is furthest from cover
  • Use the wide player to reset the angle, then attack again immediately
  • Defending pair: one presses, one covers — never both, never neither
  • Talk the whole way through the duel; the cover player makes the decisions

Progressions

  • Remove the rule that a neutral must be involved
  • Add a third defender so the pair have to defend an overload
  • Make the neutrals one touch to speed the resets up
