Session 16 of 50

Playing Through the Thirds

In Possession • Full Pitch • 6v6 (+3)

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Diagram

Objective

Link build-up, progression and penetration into one continuous action, choosing the right moment to move the ball from one third into the next.

Setup

  • Full width area split into three zones, with a goal and goalkeeper at each end
  • 6v6 plus three neutral players who always play for the team in possession
  • A goal only counts if the ball has been played through all three zones

Key coaching points

  • Secure the ball in the first third before trying to progress — patience earns the pass forward
  • Look to break a line with every pass; if it isn't on, recirculate and go again
  • One player is always available behind the ball as the safe outlet
  • Recognise the trigger to go: a defender turned, or a gap between their lines

Common mistakes

  • Players stand behind defenders instead of creating clear passing lines.
  • The ball moves faster than the support, leaving the receiver isolated.
  • Players force a forward pass when the better decision is to secure possession and move the opponent.

What good looks like

  • The player on the ball has at least two useful options.
  • Support arrives at useful angles and distances, not simply close to the ball.
  • Players recognise when to play forward and when to recycle.

Coach's language

  • “Can you give me two options?”
  • “Open your body before the ball arrives.”
  • “Can we move them before we play through?”
  • “Secure it, then progress.”

Progressions

  • Limit the first third to six passes before the ball must be progressed
  • Remove the all-three-zones rule once the pattern is understood
  • Add a touch limit in the final third to sharpen decision-making

Age groups and numbers

Best fit: U11–U16 · Group: 6–18+

Use the stated numbers as the base and adjust space before adding complexity.

Shaun's coaching eye

This practice rewards patience. I would keep asking whether the forward pass is actually on rather than praising progression for its own sake. Secure the first third, move the opponent, then play through when the gap appears.

Coach's approach
