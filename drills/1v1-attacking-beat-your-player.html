1v1 Attacking – Beat Your Player

In Possession • Attacking Third • 1v1

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Diagram

Objective

Build the confidence and technique to take defenders on in wide and central areas, committing them and getting past on either side.

Setup

  • 18m x 14m grid with a two-cone gate at each end of the far line
  • Attacker starts on the ball facing a defender who begins 8m away
  • Attacker scores by dribbling through either gate under control

Key coaching points

  • Attack the defender at speed, then change pace at the moment of the move
  • Commit the defender by running at their front foot before going the other way
  • Use one clear move — a stepover, a feint, or a knock-and-go — not three
  • Accelerate away afterwards; the first two steps past a defender matter most

Common mistakes

  • The final action is rushed before the advantage has been created.
  • Players attack the same space instead of stretching the defence.
  • The supporting player arrives too early and removes the passing option.

What good looks like

  • Players create a clear advantage before attacking the target area.
  • Runs have purpose and timing rather than simply movement for movement’s sake.
  • The final action is taken at speed once the opportunity appears.

Coach's language

  • “Can you create the advantage first?”
  • “Stretch them.”
  • “Arrive, don’t wait.”
  • “Now — attack the space.”

Progressions

  • Give the attacker a 6-second time limit per repetition
  • Start the attacker with their back to goal so they must turn first
  • Make it 1v1 to a full goal with a goalkeeper

Age groups and numbers

Best fit: U8–U12 · Group: 4–16+

Keep the area generous and reduce pressure before adding extra rules.

Coach's eye

Don't coach everything at once. Pick the one or two behaviours that matter most, then change the space or pressure before adding more rules.

Session rhythm

Coach's approach

Coach the detail that changes the game, then get the players back playing. If the practice is not working, change the space before adding another rule.
