Mid-Block Pressing – Screening the Pass

Out of Possession • Middle Third • 8v8

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Defend in a mid-block that shuts down the central options first, forcing the opposition into the areas the team wants them to play in.

Setup

  • Middle third area: the defending team in a compact 4-4 block, the attacking team in a 3-4-1
  • The attacking team scores by playing into a striker in the far zone
  • The defending team scores by winning the ball and hitting a target zone in behind

Key coaching points

  • Front players use their body to screen the pass into midfield, not to chase the ball
  • Stay compact centrally and let the ball go wide — that is the invitation, not a mistake
  • Press hard the moment the ball goes wide; the touchline is an extra defender
  • Nobody steps out of the block alone — the line pushes up together or it holds

Progressions

  • Allow a switch of play so the block must slide and reset
  • Add a second striker to test the discipline of the two centre-backs
  • Double reward for a turnover that leads to a shot
