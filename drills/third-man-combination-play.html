Third-Man Combination Play

In Possession • Attacking Third • Breaking Lines

By Shaun Ellison · FA Level 2 · Assistant Coach, Turton FCPublished August 2026 · updated August 2026

Practise combination play — a lay-off followed by a third-man run — to break defensive lines and create clear chances.

Setup

  • Half-pitch area with a target goal
  • Central player plays into a wide player who lays it off first-time
  • A third player times a run beyond the lay-off into the space created

Key coaching points

  • Disguise the pass before releasing it
  • Weight the final ball into space ahead of the runner, not to feet
  • Third-man run is timed to arrive, not to wait and then run
  • First-time lay-offs wherever the picture allows it

Progressions

  • Introduce a passive defender, then a live one
  • Count consecutive combinations ending in a shot on target
  • Restrict the lay-off player to one touch
